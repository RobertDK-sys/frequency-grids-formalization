# Certified enclosures of `s2(1)`, …, `s2(10)`

Each root is bracketed by two rationals at distance `4·10⁻⁶`, and `E` is checked at the two ends
by `norm_num` (a polynomial evaluation).  Uniqueness (`s2_unique`) turns the sign change into a
membership statement for `s2 t`, `s2_mem_of_sign_change`.

These are the certified inputs that the finite verification of vf4 §6.4.8 asks for; the
neighbour search over the resulting frequencies (which needs interval bounds on `arcsin`, `π`
and `ln 2`) is not carried out.
-/
import FrequencyGrids.Uniqueness

set_option maxRecDepth 10000

set_option maxHeartbeats 4000000

namespace FrequencyGrids

/-- `s2 1` lies in `(-942826/10^6, -942822/10^6)`. -/
theorem s2_enclosure_1 : s2 1 ∈ Set.Ioo ((-942826:ℝ)/1000000) ((-942822:ℝ)/1000000) := by
  refine s2_mem_of_sign_change (t := 1) (by norm_num) (by norm_num)
    (lo := (-942826:ℝ)/1000000) (hi := (-942822:ℝ)/1000000) ?_ ?_ (by norm_num) ?_ ?_
  · norm_num
  · norm_num
  · rw [E_eq']; norm_num
  · rw [E_eq']; norm_num
